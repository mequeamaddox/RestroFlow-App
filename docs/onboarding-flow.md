# Company and employee onboarding

Company owners register on the website. An owner without an active subscription is directed to subscription checkout before normal dashboard access. The company setup route remains available before payment; it does not grant paid operational access or create a trial.

The setup wizard saves restaurant details, creates departments and positions, optionally activates the paid HR add-on for selected owned restaurants, then creates employee invitations. Department and position identifiers are stored in the saved wizard answers so refreshes and retries preserve the links. Local setup writes and progress updates share a transaction. Failed restaurant creation or invalid staff assignments leave the step unfinished. Completion checks the real restaurant and organization records and requires every step to be completed or explicitly skipped. Old unfinished wizard answers without real identifiers resume at the first missing organization step.

HR activation uses the same Stripe synchronization service as restaurant settings and Platform Admin. The wizard displays the monthly per-restaurant price and explains prorated charges. Failed payment does not enable HR or advance setup. Retrying uses a stable billing request key. When several restaurants are selected, successful activations stay enabled and a retry completes the remaining restaurants. HR is a bundle; selected features in the wizard record setup preferences, rather than separate subscriptions.

Employee invitations preserve restaurant, department, position and pay details. A position must belong to the selected department. Repeating a wizard invitation reuses its valid pending token; employees who already accepted are not invited again. If delivery fails, the wizard displays links to copy and share before finishing. Account invitation links expire after seven days. Staff invitation roles are employee, team lead, front of house manager, kitchen manager and general manager, limited by the inviter's authority.

An employee opens the invitation link, creates an account or signs in with the invited email, then joins the restaurant. The employee profile, scoped membership and invitation acceptance commit together. Pre-created employee profiles are updated rather than duplicated. Disabled accounts, inactive restaurants and disabled owners cannot accept. Existing owners invited as staff retain their own identity and gain only the invited restaurant membership.

If that restaurant has HR enabled, acceptance creates a three-day employee information link and opens it automatically. The link works for signed-in and signed-out users. Profile completion encrypts sensitive fields using the existing PII encryption service and commits profile data and token consumption together. A failure leaves the link retryable. After saving, the employee can sign in on the website or Android app. Assigned HR documents and employer-specific checklists remain managed through the existing HR module; completing the personal information form does not assert that every document is finished.

Android currently handles sign-in, restaurant selection and operational screens. Company registration, invitation acceptance and HR information forms run on the website. These onboarding changes require a website/backend deployment, not a new APK. Configure Clerk, Resend, Stripe prices and the existing PII_ENCRYPTION_KEY before live onboarding. Existing customer billing is not changed by deployment.

Validation covers transaction rollback, retry duplication, scoped invitations, manager roles, inactive accounts, encrypted profile storage, one-time link consumption and company HTTP routes, including failed email and payment outcomes. External payment, identity creation and email delivery are stubbed during automated tests; live end-to-end signup still requires a controlled production test.
